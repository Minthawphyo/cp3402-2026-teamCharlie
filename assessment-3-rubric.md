## Description

Work in a team of **4 people** to develop and publish a **WordPress website for a client**.

You will:

- Create your own **custom theme** (PHP, HTML, CSS, optionally JavaScript)
- Write **industry-style documentation** so a new developer can continue theme development and deploy the site
- Create and use a **modern collaborative development and deployment workflow** (source control, staging, automation)
- Publish a **LinkedIn report** describing the workflow and skills gained
- Record a **5–10 minute presentation** demonstrating and justifying your workflow, comparing alternatives tested
- Include a **live demo** of using the workflow to update a live site

Peer assessments apply. Underperforming individuals may receive reduced marks based on peer evaluations and evidence of involvement.

> Marks are split evenly between **product** (the website) and **professional development process**. Process criteria are assessed from the repo, commit history, hosting, and project management tools. Markers look for **progressive development**, not last-minute assembly.
